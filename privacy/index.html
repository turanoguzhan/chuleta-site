Chuleta

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026 · Version 1.0

Overview

Chuleta is an iPhone app for learning the Spanish phrases people really use in Spain. It works offline: there is no account, no server and no analytics. What you study stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

The app keeps these on your iPhone only. They are never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • your study progress, your answers and your daily streak
  • the phrases you save
  • your settings: daily goal, categories, reminder times, audio and appearance

The phrases, pictures and explanations are part of the app itself, so nothing is downloaded while you study.

What we don't collect

Chuleta does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, advertising ID or usage analytics. It contains no third-party SDKs and doesn't track you across apps or websites.

Audio

Phrases are spoken by the Spanish voices built into iOS. The speech is made on your iPhone; no text or audio leaves it.

Reminders and widget

The practice reminder and the phrase of the day are local notifications, scheduled on your iPhone. No push server is involved.

The home-screen widget reads the upcoming phrases of the day, and their pictures, from a shared folder on your iPhone. That folder never leaves the device.

When you write to us

"Report a problem" and "Contact us" open an email draft in your own mail app, addressed to us. A report includes the phrase it's about, where in the app you saw it, the app version, the iOS version and the iPhone model, and it may attach the phrase's picture. You can read and change everything before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

We use these emails only to answer you and to fix the phrase. Ask us and we'll delete your email.

Sharing a phrase

A shared phrase card is made on your iPhone and goes only where you choose in the share menu.

Backups

If iCloud Backup is on, your iPhone's backup (Chuleta's progress included) may be stored in iCloud under your own Apple Account. That is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Me › Reset progress clears your progress and streak (your settings and saved phrases stay). Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

Chuleta collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If a later version of the app changes any of this, for example by adding in-app purchases or ads, this policy is updated before that version is released. The current version is always on this page and in the app (Me › Privacy policy).

Contact
