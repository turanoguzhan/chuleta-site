More than a translation

Each phrase tells you when to use it, who you'd say it to, and the things a dictionary leaves out.

¿Me pones un café?

Can I get a coffee?

WHEN TO USE

Ordering at a bar counter — the normal way to ask.

IN SPAIN

“¿Me pones…?” is how Spaniards order: direct and friendly. In a bar, a long “could I possibly have” sounds odd. Add “por favor” and a smile.

LITERALLY

“Do you put me a coffee?”
